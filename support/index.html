資料存在哪裡？

只存在你的手機上，不會上傳。詳見隱私權政策。

換手機怎麼搬資料？

在舊手機「設定 → 匯出資料」存下檔案，到新手機「設定 → 匯入資料」選這個檔案。

已經買過，換手機要再買嗎？

不用。用同一個帳號登入，在「設定」按「恢復購買」即可。

沒收到提醒？

到手機的「設定 → 通知 → Kiiick」確認已允許通知。

Support

Questions or feedback? Email janechiangccy@gmail.com

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. It is never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move to a new phone?

On your old phone, go to Settings → Export data and save the file. On the new phone, go to Settings → Import data and pick that file.

Do I need to buy again on a new phone?

No. Sign in with the same account and tap Restore purchases in Settings.

Not getting reminders?

Check Settings → Notifications → Kiiick on your phone and make sure notifications are allowed.
